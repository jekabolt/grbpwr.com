# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Fashion / streetwear shoppers and followers of the grbpwr label. They arrive from
social, drops, and editorial; context is mixed mobile and desktop. The core job is to
browse the catalog, understand a garment (images, sizing, composition, care), and
complete a purchase. A secondary audience manages orders and account details, and
browses the archive/timeline as editorial content.

## Product Purpose
grbpwr.com is the brand's storefront and archive. It sells current product and presents
the label's identity and history (timeline). Success = a confident, fast path from
discovery to checkout that never breaks the brand's mood, plus reliable post-purchase
flows (order lookup, account, returns).

## Brand Personality
Brutalist, minimal, art-forward, confident. Monochrome black/white with two committed
accents (electric blue `#311eee`, acid yellow `#cde100`). Tiny 12px functional base type
against giant display type. Restraint and precision, no decoration for its own sake.
Three words: severe, precise, deliberate.

## Anti-references
Generic Shopify/DTC templates. Cream/sand "editorial-warm" palettes. Rounded soft cards,
drop-shadow ghost cards, gradient accents, pill-everything. Friendly SaaS chrome. Any
move that reads as a default theme rather than an authored object.

## Design Principles
1. **Identity is non-negotiable.** Polish fixes defects (contrast, states, alignment,
   a11y); it never softens the brutalist visual language.
2. **The garment is the hero.** Chrome stays quiet so imagery and product carry the page.
3. **Functional rigor under an art surface.** Commerce flows (cart, checkout, account)
   get full product-grade state coverage even though the skin is editorial.
4. **Precision over decoration.** Every gap, weight, and state is deliberate; nothing is
   there to look busy.
5. **Accessible severity.** Minimal does not mean inaccessible: contrast, focus, and
   touch targets meet the bar without compromising the look.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Known gaps to close: visible keyboard focus (currently stripped
globally), contrast of muted/inactive text and disabled states, touch-target sizing,
and `prefers-reduced-motion` coverage for the threshold/loading animations.

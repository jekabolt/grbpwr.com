## MEMBERSHIP REQUIREMENTS

The membership is personal and shall be automatically created and registered to your email address upon registration on our website. You must be at least 16 years old. The membership is free of charge.
The membership provider is GRBPWR Limited, a company incorporated under the laws of the United Kingdom, with registration number 17015705.

## MEMBERSHIP TIERS AND BENEFITS

A "tier" means a membership status level within our loyalty programme, assigned to each member based on qualifying spend. Your tier determines which membership services and advantages may be made available to you.

Tier levels, qualifying criteria, and associated benefits are determined by us at our sole discretion and may be updated from time to time. Details of your current tier and available benefits are shown in your account.

Each tier remains valid for 12 months from the date it is assigned or upgraded. If, at the end of the relevant 12-month period, you have not met the required spend for your current tier, your tier may be downgraded. However, no downgrade will take effect earlier than 12 months from the date of your most recent upgrade.

If your email address is linked to previous purchases, qualifying spend made during the previous 12 months may be taken into account when your initial tier is assigned.

## MADE-TO-ORDER (MTO) ELIGIBILITY

Certain exclusive services, including custom-made items, are available exclusively to members who have reached the required tier level. If you are eligible, you will see an additional option in your account to request a custom order. A consultation process will follow, during which we will collect your measurements and agree on the garment specification. Payment for custom-made items is required in full upfront. Custom-made items cannot be returned. If you cancel your order after production has begun, no refund will be provided.
